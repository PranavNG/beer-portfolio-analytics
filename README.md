# Beer Portfolio Optimization Dashboard

A Power BI business analytics project focused on evaluating and optimizing a national beer product portfolio using sales, profitability, product, customer, and regional data.

This project was completed as part of **INFS5700 – Introduction to Business Analytics at UNSW** as a five-member group project. My primary contribution was **data modelling and development of the final Power BI dashboard**.

## Project Overview

The project addressed a key business question:

> **How can the company optimize its product portfolio?**

The analysis was designed to help management understand which products should be promoted, expanded, repositioned, or rationalized. The final Power BI dashboard brings together product, transaction, customer, and other business data to support analysis across revenue, profit, margins, sales volume, customer segments, regions, product quality, and discounting.

## Dashboard Highlights

The dashboard analyzes a portfolio of **150 products (SKUs)** and **3,352 customers**, covering approximately:

- **$4.31M** total revenue
- **$1.81M** total profit
- **42.1%** overall profit margin
- **561K** units sold

The analysis explores several areas of portfolio performance, including:

- Revenue and profitability by beer family
- Product quality and review scores
- ABV categories
- On-premise vs. off-premise performance
- Customer segment performance
- Regional performance
- Discount levels and their relationship with profit margins
- Individual SKU performance

## Key Insights

Some of the major findings from the team analysis included:

- High sales volume did not always correspond to the highest profit margins.
- Lager/Pilsner and Wheat products achieved margins of up to **47.2%**, while IPA and Ale products were major revenue contributors.
- **93% of revenue** came from products with review scores of 3.5 or higher.
- On-premise sales represented a smaller share of revenue but generated a higher margin than off-premise sales.
- Hospitality Buyers had the highest average revenue per customer.
- Southwest and Mountain were identified as comparatively underperforming regions.
- Profit margin declined as discount levels increased, from approximately **45.1% with no discount to 35.4% at discounts above 10%**.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the team's analysis, recommendations included:

- Continue promoting high-revenue IPA and Ale products.
- Increase focus on higher-margin Lager/Pilsner and Wheat products.
- Expand on-premise and B2B channels.
- Explore growth opportunities in underperforming regions.
- Reposition or rationalize low-performing and low-reviewed products.
- Use higher discount levels selectively rather than applying them to strong-performing products.
